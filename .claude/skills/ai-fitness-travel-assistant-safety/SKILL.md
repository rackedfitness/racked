# AI Fitness and Travel Assistant Safety

## Mandatory safeguards
- Ground gym-equipment claims in verified hotel records; label unverified information and provide corrections/reporting.
- Ask for fitness level, equipment and constraints without requiring health diagnoses. Offer conservative, adaptable workouts and stop/seek-help warnings for concerning symptoms.
- Do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or claim medical clearance; support safer alternatives and human escalation.
- Minimise/redact identity, GPS, stay details and health data in model prompts, traces and logs; assess provider retention, training use, transfer locations and subprocessors.
- Treat hotel listings, reviews, webpages and user content as untrusted; defend against prompt injection and unauthorised tool calls.
- Require user confirmation for actions that share data, contact others, purchase, book or change settings. No silent sharing.
- Add evaluation cases for unsafe exercise, fabricated equipment, discriminatory advice, privacy leaks, adversarial inputs and excessive confidence.
- Explain that AI suggestions may be inaccurate and show the basis for personalised recommendations.
## Verification
Run red-team tests and document model/provider version, known limits, human review routes and incident response.

## Boundaries
This skill is guidance, not a certification or substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal, security or clinical review.
