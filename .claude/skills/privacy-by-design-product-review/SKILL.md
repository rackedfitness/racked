# Privacy by Design Product Review

## Feature review workflow
1. Describe users, purpose, data categories, recipients, locations, retention and expected benefit.
2. Map data flows and classify personal, precise location, itinerary, social graph and potential special-category health data.
3. Identify controller/processor roles, lawful basis, transparency and PECR/cookie implications; do not use consent as a blanket substitute for other legal bases.
4. Screen for DPIA triggers: systematic tracking, profiling, sensitive data, large-scale processing, vulnerable users, new technologies, or combined datasets. Escalate high residual risk before launch.
5. Threat-model account takeover, stalking, inference, scraping, data breach, third-party misuse and AI leakage.
6. Define privacy defaults, access controls, minimisation, retention, user rights, international transfers and vendor due diligence.
7. Record decisions, evidence, test cases, owner, residual risk and release approval.
## Required output
Provide: feature summary; data-flow table; legal/technical questions; threats and mitigations; DPIA screening; acceptance criteria; open risks and accountable owner.
## Stop conditions
Do not mark launch-ready if legal basis, special-category condition (where needed), user safety controls, security tests or high-risk DPIA assessment remain unresolved.

## Boundaries
This skill is guidance, not a certification or substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal, security or clinical review.
