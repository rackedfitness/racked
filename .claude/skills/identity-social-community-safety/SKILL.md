# Identity Social and Community Safety

## Mandatory safeguards
- Pseudonyms supported; no public profile, contact discoverability or travel presence by default.
- User-specific objects require server-side ownership and relationship checks; no IDOR, enumeration or unauthorised data export.
- Require explicit mutual opt-in for connections and visibility; allow block, report, mute, unfriend and privacy controls.
- Blocking must immediately revoke visibility to location shares, profile details, conversations and future discovery where feasible.
- No searchable live hotel check-ins, room details or precise whereabouts. Do not reveal hidden data via notifications or metadata.
- Provide abuse reporting, moderation escalation, evidence minimisation and retention, appeal routes and emergency response playbooks.
- Define age policy and protections before launch; if children can access the service, perform age-appropriate design and safeguarding review.
## Verification
Test stalking and harassment scenarios, blocked-user access, invitation abuse, scraping, enumeration and privilege escalation.

## Boundaries
This skill is guidance, not a certification or substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal, security or clinical review.
