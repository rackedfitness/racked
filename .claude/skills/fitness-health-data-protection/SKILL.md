# Fitness and Health Data Protection

## Mandatory safeguards
- Collect minimum necessary data; separate equipment/preferences from health information and clearly mark optional fields.
- Classify medical details and health-revealing measurements/inferences for UK/EU GDPR special-category assessment; establish an Article 6 basis and, where applicable, Article 9 condition before processing.
- Do not assume all workout records are automatically special-category data: assess content, context and inferences.
- Keep health data private, encrypt in transit/at rest, restrict access, and avoid inclusion in analytics, ads, logs or AI prompts without necessity and appropriate controls.
- Provide clear notices, purpose limitations, retention schedules, access/export/deletion and secure backup expiry.
- Avoid health diagnoses and treatment claims; refer high-risk conditions and acute symptoms to qualified professionals.
- Treat wearable imports as separately permissioned, with revocation and deletion flows.
## Verification
Test cross-account isolation, data deletion across derived features and providers, consent withdrawal where consent is the basis, and sensitive-data leakage.

## Boundaries
This skill is guidance, not a certification or substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal, security or clinical review.
